Relax, Explore, Enjoy

Nestled in a beautiful valley in the Swiss Alps, our resort is the perfect escape from the ordinary. Dining options include an Italian restaurant, and ski-in bars on mountainside terraces. On-site amenities include hot tubs, a gym and a climbing wall. Breakfast is available.

Amenities and Options for Everyone

Escape from everyday life and spend a few magical days with us. Discover the village and the surrounding mountains, relax by the fire, or take a guided hike during the warmer months.

Unforgettable Scenery

Whether you're enjoying the sunset over a tranquil lake or waking up to snow-dusted peaks, the Alpine Bliss Resort surrounds you with unforgettable natural beauty in every season.
